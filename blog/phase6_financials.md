# Phase 6: Financial Analysis & Health Economics of Cemiplimab

## Introduction
Monoclonal antibody therapies represent a major healthcare cost. In this post, we analyze the financial dynamics of cemiplimab (Libtayo®), comparing geographic pricing, annual revenues, and published cost-effectiveness evaluations.

---

## 1. Geographic Pricing and Treatment Costs
Because different national healthcare systems structure pricing and reimbursement differently, cemiplimab exhibits significant variation:
*   **United States:** wholesale Acquisition Cost (WAC) list price is **$11,703.03** per 350 mg vial.
*   **United Kingdom:** NHS list price is **£4,650** per vial (subject to a confidential Patient Access Scheme discount).
*   **Australia:** PBS dispensed price is approximately **$7,381.23 AUD**.
*   **Japan:** NHI price is **450,437 JPY**.
*   **1-Year Adjuvant Cost:** Based on a standard 17-cycle course (350 mg Q3W), list cost ranges from **$51,304** (Japan, exchange-rate normalized) to **$198,951** (US WAC).

---

## 2. Sales Performance & Blockbuster Status
Following the Sanofi transaction, Regeneron records 100% of global product sales:
*   **2023 Revenue:** Global net sales of **$868.8 million USD**.
*   **2024 Revenue:** Global net sales of **$1,217.0 million USD**.
*   **YoY Growth:** A **40.1% growth rate**, driven by expanding uptake and label expansions.

---

## 3. Health Economics: Cost-Effectiveness
To secure single-payer reimbursement, cemiplimab must prove cost-effective:
*   **Model Structure:** Evaluations utilize a 3-state partitioned survival model (Progression-Free, Progressive Disease, Death) over a 30-year lifetime horizon.
*   **Utility Values:** High quality-of-life scores are maintained during therapy (PFS utility = 0.805 vs. PD utility = 0.749).
*   **ICER:** In the US, cemiplimab vs. chemotherapy yields an Incremental Cost-Effectiveness Ratio (ICER) of **$99,024 per QALY gained**, which is well below the standard $150,000 willingness-to-pay threshold.

---

## Conclusion
Despite high list costs, cemiplimab exhibits robust commercial revenue growth and meets established willingness-to-pay cost-effectiveness thresholds globally.
