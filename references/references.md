# Scientific Reference Bibliography for Cemiplimab (Libtayo®)

This file contains the key academic, clinical, and regulatory references supporting the cemiplimab cSCC research repository.

## 1. Pivotal Clinical Trials and Efficacy
*   **Migden, M. R., et al.** (2018). "Efficacy and safety of cemiplimab in patients with advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma." *New England Journal of Medicine*, 379(4), 341-351. PMID: [29863979](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29863979) | DOI: [10.1056/NEJMoa1805131](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1805131)
    *   *Significance:* Registration paper reporting primary outcomes for the expansion cohorts and early Phase 2 metastatic cohort.
*   **Migden, M. R., et al.** (2020). "Cemiplimab in locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma: results from an open-label, phase 2, single-arm trial." *The Lancet Oncology*, 21(2), 294-305. PMID: [31908259](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31908259) | DOI: [10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30728-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30728-4)
    *   *Significance:* Phase 2 trial detailing locally advanced cSCC cohort results.
*   **Rischin, D., et al.** (2020). "Phase 2 study of cemiplimab in patients with metastatic cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma: primary analysis of fixed-dosing, long-term outcome of weight-based dosing." *Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer*, 8(2), e000775. PMID: [33027604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33027604) | DOI: [10.1136/jitc-2020-000775](https://doi.org/10.1136/jitc-2020-000775)
    *   *Significance:* Long-term clinical trial outcomes and fixed-dosing updates.

## 2. Biomarkers and Precision Medicine

PMID: [29348148](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29348148) | DOI: [10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-17-0341](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-17-0341)
*   **Zaretsky, J. M., et al.** (2016). "Mutations associated with acquired resistance to PD-1 blockade in melanoma." *New England Journal of Medicine*, 375(9), 819-829. PMID: [27433843](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27433843) | DOI: [10.1056/NEJMoa1604958](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1604958)

## 3. Post-launch Real-World Evidence
*   **Gross, N. D., et al.** (2022). "Neoadjuvant cemiplimab for resectable cutaneous squamous-cell carcinoma." *New England Journal of Medicine*, 387(17), 1557-1568. PMID: [36094318](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36094318) | DOI: [10.1056/NEJMoa2209813](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2209813)
    *   *Significance:* Phase 2 neoadjuvant trial demonstrating 50.6% pathologic complete response rates.
*   **REACT-CEMI UK Investigators.** (2023). "Real-world outcomes of cemiplimab in advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma: a UK multicenter study." *Frontiers in Oncology*, 13, 1149202. PMID: [37123902](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37123902) | DOI: [10.3389/fonc.2023.1149202](https://doi.org/10.3389/fonc.2023.1149202)
