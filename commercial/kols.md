# Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) and Scientific Leadership for Cemiplimab

## 1. Background
Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) and principal investigators (PIs) play a pivotal role in the biopharmaceutical lifecycle. By leading clinical trials, presenting data at major medical congresses, and authoring peer-reviewed publications, these scientific leaders shape clinical consensus and influence physician adoption. This document summarizes the key academic and clinical investigators who have driven cemiplimab’s development in cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cSCC).

---

## 2. Key Investigators and Authors

### 2.1 Dr. Danny Rischin, MD
*   **Affiliation:** Director of Medical Oncology, Peter MacCallum Cancer Centre, Melbourne, Australia.
*   **Role:** Lead investigator and primary author of the pivotal EMPOWER-CSCC-1 Phase 2 clinical trial publications (e.g., in *Journal of Clinical Oncology* and *Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer*).
*   **Impact:** A leading international voice in head and neck cancers and skin oncology. Dr. Rischin has presented long-term cemiplimab follow-up data at major congresses (ASCO and ESMO), confirming the durability of response and complete response rates.

### 2.2 Dr. Michael R. Migden, MD
*   **Affiliation:** Professor of Dermatology and Head and Neck Surgery, MD Anderson Cancer Center, Houston, Texas, USA.
*   **Role:** Principal investigator and lead author of the pivotal Phase 2 trial publication in *The Lancet Oncology* (2020) focusing on locally advanced cSCC.
*   **Impact:** Bridges dermatologic surgery (Mohs) and medical oncology, serving as a key facilitator of interdisciplinary adoption.

### 2.3 Dr. Chrysalyne D. Schmults, MD, MS
*   **Affiliation:** Associate Professor of Dermatology, Brigham and Women's Hospital, Boston, Massachusetts, USA.
*   **Role:** Co-investigator on cemiplimab trials and lead developer of the Brigham and Women's Hospital (BWH) staging system for high-risk cSCC.
*   **Impact:** Influences guideline development and risk-stratification criteria for adjuvant therapies.

---

## 3. Scientific Congress Presentations

Major oncology congresses represent key commercial milestones where data updates are presented:
*   **American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) Annual Meeting:** Dr. Rischin and colleagues have presented successive updates on the EMPOWER-CSCC-1 cohorts, focusing on long-term overall survival and Complete Response durability.
*   **European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) Congress:** Key European and international PIs have presented safety profiles, patient-reported quality of life (QoL) metrics, and subgroup analyses (e.g., age-stratified outcomes).
*   **European Association of Dermato-Oncology (EADO) Congress:** Platform for discussing interdisciplinary management and the transition from surgical oncology to systemic immunotherapy.
