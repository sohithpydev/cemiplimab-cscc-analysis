# Libtayo® (Cemiplimab) Commercial Strategy and Sales Performance

## 1. Background
Cemiplimab (Libtayo®) is a fully human IgG4 anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody. Initially approved by the US FDA in September 2018, cemiplimab represented the first systemic therapy specifically indicated for patients with metastatic or locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cSCC) who are not candidates for curative surgery or radiation. The commercialization strategy for Libtayo has focused on defining a leadership position in non-melanoma skin cancers (cSCC and BCC) while selectively expanding into larger oncology indications like Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC).

## 2. Regeneron-Sanofi Launch and Sales Performance

### 2.1 Launch Timeline and Geographic Rollout
*   **United States:** Launched in October 2018 following FDA approval. Regeneron led US commercial operations, co-promoting with Sanofi.
*   **European Union:** Approved by the European Commission in June 2019 as the first approved systemic treatment for advanced cSCC. Sanofi led commercial operations in Europe and international markets.
*   **Rest of World:** Rolled out in other key markets (e.g., Japan, Canada, Australia) starting in 2020.

### 2.2 Sales Growth (2023 vs. 2024)
Following the July 2022 buyout agreement, Regeneron assumed full responsibility for global product sales, paying Sanofi an 11% royalty. Regeneron's commercial execution has driven rapid growth:
*   **Full Year 2023:** Global net product sales of **$868.8 million USD**.
*   **Full Year 2024:** Global net product sales of **$1,217.0 million USD**.
*   **Year-on-Year Growth:** This represents a **40.1% increase** in global revenue, driven by increased penetration in advanced NSCLC and the expansion into the adjuvant cSCC setting.

## 3. Indication Expansion Strategy

Libtayo’s commercial success is anchored in a stepwise lifecycle expansion:
1.  **Cutaneous Squamous Cell Carcinoma (cSCC) - 2018:** Secured the first-mover advantage in advanced cSCC, establishing a 90%+ market share in this niche.
2.  **Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC) - 2021:** Approved for patients with locally advanced or metastatic BCC previously treated with or ineligible for a hedgehog pathway inhibitor (HPI).
3.  **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) - 2021/2022:** Approved as monotherapy (first-line, high PD-L1 $\ge$ 50%) and in combination with chemotherapy (first-line, squamous or non-squamous, regardless of PD-L1 level).
4.  **Adjuvant high-risk cSCC - 2025:** Approved as the first immunotherapy for high-risk cSCC patients in the post-surgical adjuvant setting, expanding the treatable patient pool to earlier stages of disease.
