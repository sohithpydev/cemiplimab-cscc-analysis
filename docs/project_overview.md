# Project Overview and Scientific Scope

## 1. Project Background
This repository serves as a comprehensive, publication-quality research resource dedicated to **Cemiplimab (Libtayo®)** in the treatment of Cutaneous Squamous Cell Carcinoma (cSCC). 

Immunotherapies targeting the programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) pathway have transformed the management of advanced malignancies. Cemiplimab, a fully human IgG4 monoclonal antibody, holds a pioneering position as the first systemic treatment approved for patients with advanced (metastatic or locally advanced) cSCC who are not candidates for curative surgery or radiotherapy, and subsequently as the first approved adjuvant therapy for high-risk resected disease.

---

## 2. Multi-Disciplinary Focus Areas
The project is structured into seven distinct scientific, regulatory, and business modules:
1.  **Mechanism of Action & Structural Biology:** Evaluates PD-1/PD-L1 pathway binding, molecular kinetics ($K_D$), glycosylation dependencies (PDB 7WVM and 8GY5), and pharmacokinetics (elimination half-life of 20–22 days).
2.  **Clinical Efficacy (RCTs):** Analyzes efficacy benchmarks from the pivotal EMPOWER-CSCC-1 Phase 2 trial (ORR 47.2%, CR 16.1%) and the randomized adjuvant Phase 3 C-POST trial (68% recurrence risk reduction).
3.  **Clinical Development & Safety:** Examines subgroup responses (maintaining efficacy in the elderly $\ge$ 75 years) and profiles treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), immune-related toxicities (irAEs), and patient discontinuation rates (approx. 9.8%).
4.  **Regulatory History:** Documents FDA and EMA label evolution, breakthrough designations, and the Real-Time Oncology Review (RTOR) pilot program.
5.  **Precision Biomarkers:** Reviews PD-L1 expression clinical utility, Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) distributions (median 53–61 mut/Mb in responders vs. 13–19 mut/Mb in non-responders), tumor immune microenvironments, and acquired resistance pathways (B2M loss, JAK1/2 mutations).
6.  **Intellectual Property & Exclusivity:** Analyzes the patent landscape (US composition, formulation, and method-of-use patents extending to 2035–2038), regulatory data exclusivity (BPCIA 12 years US, EMA 10 years EU), licensing buyout terms ($900M upfront, 11% royalty), and biosimilar barriers.
7.  **Financials & Health Economics:** Compiles international list pricing (US WAC $11,703.03/vial), reimbursement HTA decisions (NICE routine NHS commissioning, Australia PBS S100), and cost-effectiveness partitioned survival models (US ICER $99,024/QALY gained vs. chemotherapy).
8.  **Post-launch & RWE:** Validates trial efficacy via large real-world cohorts (CASE registry, REACT-CEMI, US Database), pharmacovigilance reports (FAERS myocarditis signals), and future combinations (anti-LAG-3 fianlimab, neoadjuvant Gross 2022 NEJM pCR rates of 50.6%).

## 3. Scientific Methodology
All data compiled in this repository is sourced from peer-reviewed primary oncology journals, regulatory licensing briefings (FDA, EMA), health technology assessments (NICE, CADTH, PBAC), and corporate financial filings (SEC Form 10-K). Visualizations are produced programmatically using Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn) to ensure 100% reproducibility and scientific transparency.
