# Cemiplimab (Libtayo®) Patent Landscape Analysis

## 1. Background
Patent portfolios are the primary mechanism for defending market share against competitors in the biopharmaceutical industry. The patent landscape for cemiplimab is built upon a multi-layered, defensive patent structure consisting of core composition-of-matter patents, method-of-use patents, and formulation/dosing patents. This document analyzes these key components of Regeneron's intellectual property portfolio.

---

## 2. Core Patent Categories

### 2.1 Composition-of-Matter Patents
Composition-of-matter patents protect the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) itself and represent the strongest form of patent protection, as they prevent competitors from manufacturing or selling the same antibody, regardless of the indication.
*   **US Patent 9,987,500:** 
    *   **Title:** *Anti-PD-1 Antibodies and Methods of Use thereof*
    *   **Filing Date:** September 18, 2015
    *   **Grant Date:** June 5, 2018
    *   **Estimated Expiration:** **September 18, 2035**
    *   **Details:** Covers the cemiplimab antibody molecule (referred to in the specifications by its sequence identifiers and clone designation H4H7798N), including heavy and light chain variable regions, CDR sequences (CDR1, CDR2, CDR3), and the recombinant expression plasmid constructs utilized in Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cell production systems.
*   **US Patent 10,737,113:**
    *   **Filing Date:** April 10, 2015
    *   **Estimated Expiration:** **April 10, 2035**
    *   **Details:** A related composition patent protecting the specific immunoglobulin G4 (IgG4) isotype sequence and functional target affinity profiles.

### 2.2 Method-of-Use Patents
Method-of-use patents protect the specific clinical application of the drug for treating particular diseases. They provide crucial secondary protection that can prevent competitors from utilizing the drug for specific indications.
*   **US Patent 10,457,725:**
    *   **Title:** *Methods of treating advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma with an anti-PD-1 antibody*
    *   **Filing Date:** May 12, 2017
    *   **Grant Date:** October 29, 2019
    *   **Estimated Expiration:** **May 12, 2037**
    *   **Details:** Covers methods of administering cemiplimab to treat patients with advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (metastatic or locally advanced) who are not candidates for surgery or radiation. This patent aligns directly with cemiplimab's first FDA approval.

### 2.3 Formulation Patents
Formulation patents protect the specific pharmaceutical carrier or buffer system that stabilizes the antibody molecule for storage and administration.
*   **US Patent 11,603,407:**
    *   **Title:** *Formulations comprising anti-PD-1 antibodies*
    *   **Filing Date:** March 21, 2018
    *   **Grant Date:** March 21, 2023
    *   **Estimated Expiration:** **March 21, 2038**
    *   **Details:** Covers stable aqueous pharmaceutical formulations comprising cemiplimab, a buffering agent (histidine), a stabilizer (sucrose), and a non-ionic surfactant (polysorbate 80), protecting the commercial vial configuration (e.g., 350 mg single-dose vials).

## 3. Geographic Coverage and Families
Regeneron has secured parallel patent protection across major global pharmaceutical jurisdictions, establishing extensive international patent families.
*   **European Union (EP 3274367):** Covers the core anti-PD-1 composition of matter in EU member states, expiring in **2035**.
*   **European Union (EP 3656784):** Method-of-treatment patent covering advanced cSCC therapies, granted in September 2022 and extending to **2037**.
*   **Japan (JP 6729831):** Composition patent granted by the Japan Patent Office (JPO) in July 2020, extending protection to **2035**.
