# Intellectual Property and Licensing Overview for Cemiplimab (Libtayo®)

## 1. Background
Cemiplimab-rwlc (Libtayo®) is a fully human anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody. Establishing robust intellectual property (IP) protection is a core business requirement for biological therapeutics due to the high costs associated with clinical development, which frequently exceed $1–2 billion. Regeneron Pharmaceuticals has built a multi-layered IP shield around cemiplimab, spanning composition-of-matter, formulation, manufacturing processes, and method-of-use patents. This analysis reviews the licensing, patent strategies, and regulatory exclusivity mechanisms safeguarding Libtayo.

---

## 2. Licensing and Corporate Partnerships

The development and commercialization of cemiplimab have been governed by a deep partnership between Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. and Sanofi S.A.

### 2.1 The Immuno-Oncology Collaboration (2015)
*   **Establishment:** In July 2015, Regeneron and Sanofi entered into a broad, global immuno-oncology collaboration to co-develop and co-commercialize antibody therapeutics, including cemiplimab.
*   **Original Terms:** Under the 2015 agreement:
    *   Operating profits and losses from global commercialization were split equally (50/50).
    *   Co-commercialization activities were shared, with Sanofi leading in international markets and both sharing US efforts.
    *   Development costs were funded by both parties according to contractually agreed ratios.

### 2.2 Restructuring and Buyout (2022)
In mid-2022, the companies announced a major restructuring of their collaboration, transforming cemiplimab's financial and operational management.
*   **The Agreement (June 2, 2022):** Regeneron acquired Sanofi’s entire stake in the cemiplimab program. The transaction officially closed on July 1, 2022.
*   **Key Financial Terms:**
    *   **Upfront Payment:** Regeneron paid Sanofi **$900 million** in cash to buy out Sanofi's global rights.
    *   **Royalties:** Sanofi transitioned to receiving an **11% royalty** on all worldwide net sales of Libtayo.
    *   **Milestones:** Sanofi is entitled to a **$100 million regulatory milestone payment** upon receiving first approval in the US or EU for cemiplimab in combination with chemotherapy for first-line NSCLC (which occurred in late 2022), and up to **$100 million in sales-related milestone payments** based on net sales goals.
*   **Operational Control:** Regeneron assumed **100% of global development, manufacturing, and commercialization responsibilities**. Regeneron now records 100% of global net sales and operating expenses associated with Libtayo.

---

## 3. Current Commercial Status
Following the 2022 restructuring, Regeneron’s financial reports reflect sole commercial interest. Global net sales of Libtayo have grown steadily, driven by label expansions in cSCC, NSCLC, and BCC, with Regeneron paying the 11% royalty directly to Sanofi. This buyout allows Regeneron to consolidate its oncology franchise and integrate Libtayo with its emerging pipeline of bispecific antibodies (e.g., fianlimab, an anti-LAG-3 antibody).
