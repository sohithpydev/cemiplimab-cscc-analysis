# PD-L1 Expression in Cutaneous Squamous Cell Carcinoma (cSCC)

## 1. Background
Programmed Death-Ligand 1 (PD-L1, also known as B7-H1 or CD274) is a 40-kDa transmembrane protein that binds to the PD-1 receptor on T-cells, transmitting an inhibitory signal that dampens T-cell receptor (TCR) mediated activity. In cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cSCC), chronic ultraviolet (UV) radiation and persistent inflammatory states induce the upregulation of PD-L1 on both tumor cells and tumor-infiltrating immune cells, serving as a key mechanism of adaptive immune escape.

## 2. Expression in cSCC
PD-L1 expression in cSCC is highly prevalent compared to other non-melanoma skin cancers.
*   **Prevalence:** Studies utilizing immunohistochemistry (IHC) (e.g., using clone 22C3 or SP263) report that **30% to 50%** of advanced cSCC specimens show PD-L1 positivity (typically defined as tumor proportion score [TPS] $\ge$ 1%).
*   **Expression Patterns:** PD-L1 expression is often prominent at the invasive tumor margin (stromal-tumor interface), where tumor cells interact directly with infiltrating CD8+ T-cells.

## 3. Clinical Relevance and Predictive Value (EMPOWER-CSCC-1)
In many solid tumors (e.g., NSCLC), PD-L1 expression level is a mandated companion diagnostic that strongly correlates with immunotherapy response. However, in advanced cSCC, **PD-L1 expression has not demonstrated reliable predictive value**.

In the EMPOWER-CSCC-1 Phase 2 trial:
*   **Responses in PD-L1 Negative Tumors:** Clinical responses (including complete responses) to cemiplimab were observed in patients with PD-L1 expression <1%.
*   **Lack of Stratification Utility:** The Objective Response Rate (ORR) did not differ significantly between patients with high expression ($\ge$ 50%), low expression (1% to 49%), and negative expression (<1%).
*   **Clinical Practice:** Consequently, regulatory agencies (FDA and EMA) approved cemiplimab for advanced and adjuvant cSCC **without requiring pre-treatment PD-L1 IHC testing**.

## 4. Limitations
*   **Spatial Heterogeneity:** PD-L1 expression is highly variable within different regions of the same tumor (intratumoral heterogeneity) or between primary tumors and metastases. A single biopsy specimen may not reflect the overall PD-L1 status of the disease.
*   **Temporal Dynamics:** PD-L1 is an inducible protein. Its expression is dynamically upregulated in response to interferon-gamma (IFN-$\gamma$) secreted by activated T-cells, making baseline assessments a poor representation of post-treatment immune dynamics.
*   **Assay Discordance:** Different IHC antibodies (e.g., 22C3, 28-8, SP263, SP142) and platforms show varying sensitivity, leading to inconsistent classification of PD-L1 status.

## 5. Future Directions
To overcome the limitations of standard IHC, research is moving toward:
*   **Digital Pathology and Transcriptomics:** Implementing machine-learning algorithms to analyze tumor tissue slides to infer spatial transcriptomics and predict response based on overall microenvironment signatures rather than a single ligand count.
*   **Multiplex Immunofluorescence:** Simultaneously staining for PD-L1, PD-1, and CD8 to quantify the spatial proximity of checkpoint molecules to effector cells, which may provide higher predictive accuracy.
