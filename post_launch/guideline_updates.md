# Evolution of Clinical Guidelines for Cemiplimab in cSCC

## 1. Background
Clinical practice guidelines represent the consensus of medical experts, translating clinical trial data into standard therapeutic pathways. Since the first approval of cemiplimab in 2018, oncology and dermatology guidelines have evolved dramatically, shifting cemiplimab from a novel specialized systemic therapy to the established backbone of advanced and adjuvant cSCC treatment. This document outlines the chronological evolution of these guidelines.

---

## 2. Chronological Guideline Evolution

### 2.1 The Pre-Immunotherapy Era (Pre-2018)
*   **Landscape:** Guidelines (NCCN, ESMO) focused primarily on localized surgery and radiotherapy. Systemic options for unresectable or metastatic disease were limited to off-label platinum chemotherapy or EGFR inhibitors (cetuximab), which were designated as having low evidence levels and temporary efficacy.

### 2.2 Introduction of Cemiplimab (2018–2019)
*   **Approval & Integration:** Following the FDA approval of cemiplimab in September 2018 and EMA approval in June 2019:
    *   **NCCN (v1.2019):** Swiftly added cemiplimab-rwlc as a **preferred systemic option (Category 2A preferred)** for locally advanced or metastatic cSCC.
    *   **European Guidelines (EADO/EDF/EORTC):** Recommended cemiplimab as the first-line systemic treatment standard for advanced cSCC in patients not eligible for surgery or radiotherapy.

### 2.3 Expansion of the Class (2020–2021)
*   **Class Expansion:** Following pembrolizumab's FDA approval in June 2020:
    *   **NCCN (v2.2020):** Added pembrolizumab alongside cemiplimab as preferred Category 2A systemic therapies.
    *   **ESMO Guidelines:** Solidified anti-PD-1 monotherapy as the global standard of care in first-line systemic treatment, relegating chemotherapy and EGFR inhibitors to second-line or salvage therapy in patients ineligible for checkpoint blockade.

### 2.4 Transition to the Adjuvant Setting (2025)
*   **Adjuvant Integration:** Following the release of the C-POST clinical trial data and subsequent regulatory approvals in 2025:
    *   **NCCN (v1.2025):** Expanded the systemic algorithm to include adjuvant cemiplimab (1 year, Q3W or Q2W) as a preferred systemic option for patients with high-risk resected cSCC following definitive surgical resection and local radiotherapy.

## 3. Comparative Summary of Guideline Recommendations

| Guideline Body | Advanced/Metastatic (First-line) | Adjuvant (High-risk Resected) | Second-line (Post-PD-1 Progression) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NCCN (US)** | **Cemiplimab** or **Pembrolizumab** (Category 2A preferred) | **Cemiplimab** (Preferred) | Clinical trial, **Cetuximab**, or Platinum Chemo |
