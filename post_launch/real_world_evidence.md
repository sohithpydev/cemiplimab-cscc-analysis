# Real-World Evidence and Observational Outcomes for Cemiplimab in cSCC

## 1. Background
Randomized clinical trials (RCTs) are the gold standard for establishing drug efficacy, but they operate under highly selective inclusion/exclusion criteria. Real-world evidence (RWE) provides essential validation by characterizing a drug's performance in clinical practice, capturing heterogeneous populations (including patients with poor performance status, advanced age, and significant comorbidities) who are typically excluded from registration trials.

---

## 2. Key Observational Cohorts

Several retrospective and prospective real-world studies have validated cemiplimab’s efficacy in advanced cSCC:

### 2.1 The CASE Study
*   **Study Design:** A large, prospective, observational registry of patients with advanced cSCC treated with cemiplimab in the US and Europe.
*   **Outcomes:** Interim analyses of 254 patients demonstrated an Objective Response Rate (ORR) of **43.7%** (with an **11.2%** Complete Response [CR] rate). The median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) was **14.7 months**. These outcomes are closely aligned with the EMPOWER-CSCC-1 clinical trial data.

### 2.2 UK Multicenter Cohort (REACT-CEMI)
*   **Study Design:** A retrospective study of 105 patients across multiple NHS trusts.
*   **Outcomes:** Reported an ORR of **42.0%** (8.0% CR), a median PFS of **8.6 months**, and a median Overall Survival (OS) of **21.0 months**.

### 2.3 US Real-World Database Study
*   **Study Design:** Analysis of 622 patients in a national electronic health record database.
*   **Outcomes:** The median OS was **24.8 months** across the entire cohort. However, patients with baseline ECOG performance status $\ge$ 2 exhibited significantly worse survival, highlighting the prognostic value of functional status.

## 3. Efficacy in Special and Vulnerable Populations

### 3.1 Elderly Patients ($\ge$ 75 Years)
Because cSCC is directly caused by cumulative UV exposure, the median age at diagnosis is 72–75 years. Real-world cohorts confirm that cemiplimab maintains high efficacy (ORR > 40%) in octogenarians and nonagenarians, without an increase in severe immune-related adverse events.

### 3.2 Immunocompromised Patients
Patients who are chronically immunosuppressed (e.g., solid organ transplant recipients [SOTRs], patients with Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia [CLL], or HIV) have a 100-fold to 250-fold increased risk of developing cSCC.
*   **Solid Organ Transplant Recipients (SOTRs):** 
    *   **The Dilemma:** Using PD-1 inhibitors like cemiplimab in SOTRs carries a high risk of T-cell mediated **allograft rejection (approximately 30% to 40% rejection rate)**, which can lead to organ failure and death.
    *   **Real-world Experience:** RWE shows that cemiplimab can be used in SOTRs with strict multidisciplinary monitoring. Rejection rates match historical averages, and some patients achieve durable tumor control while maintaining graft function through adjusted immunosuppressive regimens.
*   **Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL):**
    *   **Outcomes:** Patients with co-existing CLL exhibit slightly lower response rates (ORR ~30–35%) to cemiplimab due to disease-induced T-cell dysfunction and exhaustion, though they still derive meaningful clinical benefit.
