# Cutaneous Squamous Cell Carcinoma (cSCC) Market Size and Eligible Population

## 1. Background
Cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cSCC) is the second most common form of skin cancer globally, representing approximately 20% of all non-melanoma skin malignancies. While the vast majority of cases are diagnosed at an early stage and successfully resolved with surgical excision, a small percentage of patients progress to advanced disease (locally advanced or metastatic). This document reviews the incidence, eligible treatment population, and commercial market size estimates for cemiplimab.

---

## 2. Epidemiological Market Size Estimates

### 2.1 Overall cSCC Incidence
*   **United States:** Estimates suggest that between **700,000 and 1,000,000 cases** of cSCC are diagnosed annually in the U.S. Because non-melanoma skin cancers are not tracked by standard cancer registries (such as SEER), these figures represent statistical models rather than direct case counts.
*   **Global:** Incidence is increasing worldwide, driven by aging populations, cumulative lifetime UV exposure, and increased clinical awareness.

### 2.2 Advanced and Metastatic cSCC Population
*   Approximately **3% to 5%** of patients with primary cSCC will experience disease recurrence or progression to advanced stages:
    *   **United States:** Clinical literature estimates that approximately **30,000 patients** are diagnosed with advanced cSCC (including locally advanced and metastatic stages) annually.
    *   **Mortality:** Advanced cSCC results in an estimated **3,000 to 9,000 deaths** annually in the United States, which is comparable to the mortality rate of cutaneous melanoma.

---

## 3. Eligible Systemic Treatment Population

The target commercial market for cemiplimab in the first-line systemic setting comprises patients whose tumors are **not candidates for curative surgery or curative radiation**.

### 3.1 Patient Stratification
Based on clinical consensus and registry analyses, the patient pool is segmented as follows:
1.  **Locally Advanced (approx. 60% of eligible systemic pool):** Patients with deep tissue invasion, bone involvement, or perineural infiltration where surgical resection would cause severe cosmetic or functional disfigurement (e.g., loss of eye, nose, or major facial nerves).
2.  **Metastatic (approx. 40% of eligible systemic pool):** Patients with nodal spread (parotid or cervical lymph nodes) or distant visceral metastases (lung, liver, bone).
3.  **Adjuvant High-Risk (expanded pool):** Patients who have undergone complete surgical resection of high-risk tumors but face a high risk of recurrence (margins+, perineural invasion, nodal involvement).

---

## 4. Market Trends and Commercial Outlook
The market size for cSCC immunotherapies is expanding:
*   **Aging Demographic:** cSCC is a disease of the elderly, with the median age of advanced cSCC patients being 72–75 years. As the global population ages, the incidence of advanced cases will continue to rise.
*   **Adjuvant Transition:** The approval of cemiplimab in the adjuvant setting (2025) shifts the treatment window earlier in the clinical pathway, expanding the commercial pool to patients who would otherwise have been managed by post-surgical observation alone.
