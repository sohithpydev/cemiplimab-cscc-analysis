# Libtayo® (Cemiplimab) Global Revenue and Corporate Financial Impact

## 1. Background
Cemiplimab (Libtayo®) has transitioned from an early-stage skin cancer niche therapy to a blockbuster commercial asset for Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. Analyzing Libtayo’s revenue trajectory and contribution to the corporate top-line provides critical insights into the commercial viability of anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors. This document reviews annual sales performance, growth dynamics, and financial contribution to Regeneron’s oncology franchise.

---

## 2. Global Revenue Performance

### 2.1 Annual Sales Performance (2023 vs. 2024)
Regeneron records 100% of global net product sales for Libtayo following the July 2022 restructuring of the Sanofi collaboration.
*   **Full Year 2023:** Global net product sales of **$868.8 million USD**.
*   **Full Year 2024:** Global net product sales of **$1,217.0 million USD**.
*   **Year-on-Year Growth:** This represents a **40.1% growth rate**, crossing the $1 billion blockbuster threshold for the first time.
*   **Quarterly Growth:** Quarterly sales have grown steadily (average quarter-on-quarter growth of 8–10%), driven by increased uptake in first-line advanced NSCLC.

### 2.2 Financial Contribution to Regeneron
*   **Strategic Diversification:** Historically, Regeneron’s revenue has been heavily concentrated in two major blockbuster products: **Eylea®** (aflibercept, for retinal diseases) and **Dupixent®** (dupilumab, for inflammatory diseases, co-promoted with Sanofi).
*   **Oncology Growth Engine:** Libtayo represents Regeneron’s first successful entry into the highly lucrative oncology market. It acts as the anchor asset for Regeneron's oncology franchise, supporting the clinical development of next-generation combinations, such as the anti-LAG-3 antibody fianlimab and various bispecific antibodies.

---

## 3. Impact of the 2022 Sanofi Transaction
Prior to July 2022, Regeneron and Sanofi shared cemiplimab profits and losses equally. Under the restructured terms:
*   **Net Product Sales:** Regeneron records all sales globally, which has led to a significant increase in reported product revenues for the company.
*   **Operating Margin:** Although Regeneron paid $900 million upfront and pays an 11% royalty to Sanofi, acquiring full control has improved operating margins by consolidating global manufacturing and commercialization activities under one entity.
